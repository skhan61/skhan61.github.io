  • Hangman AI Solver - Position-wise Neural Approach

    • Problem: win Hangman on 1,000 games of words never seen in training (250K-word dictionary, black-box API, 6 wrong guesses allowed) against an 18% baseline.

    • Meta-reinforcement learning (RL2): a recurrent policy that adapts within each game reached 45% on unseen words; the 26-letter action space limited sequential RL.

    • Reframed the problem after BERT’s masked language modeling: predict the letter at every hidden position. A BiLSTM trained on 21M game states from 227K words, generated with 13 masking strategies, reached a 67.2% official win rate, 3.7× the baseline.

    • Tested what else helps: heuristic baselines (letter frequency, n-grams, ensembles) stayed at 15–25%; self-supervised contrastive training added 0.8 points at 4.7× the training time, so supervised training was kept.

    • Released as Hangman AI Solver (v1.0), Zenodo, October 2025 (PyTorch Lightning, 5.2M parameters).
